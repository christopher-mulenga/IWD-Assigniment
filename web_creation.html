Welcome to Mulenga Corp

Mulenga Corp is an organisation that supports and rehabilitates people who are losing their sight or have become blind.

We help people develop skills that enable them to learn, work and live more independently.

About Us

Our Purpose

Our purpose is to help people adapt to vision loss by providing rehabilitation, technology training and accessibility education.

Our Mission

We aim to promote independence and equal access to education, communication and technology.

Our Services

  • Vision Rehabilitation
  • Accessibility Training
  • Assistive Technology Training
  • Braille Training
  • Computer Training
  • Digital Skills Training

Assistive Technology

We introduce learners to assistive technologies that make computers and mobile devices easier to use.

Examples include screen readers, magnification software, keyboard shortcuts and accessible mobile applications.

Training Programmes

Braille Training

Learners are taught Braille reading and writing skills to help them communicate and access information.

Computer Accessibility

Learners are taught how to use computers with accessibility tools such as screen readers and keyboard shortcuts.

Assistive Tools

Training may include screen readers, magnification software, accessible mobile applications and other assistive technologies.

Success Stories

"With the right training and accessibility tools, vision loss does not have to prevent a person from learning and becoming independent."

Our rehabilitation programmes help learners develop practical skills for education, employment and everyday life.
